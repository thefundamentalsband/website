Who We Are

Three chords,
one crowd.

The Fundamentals are a London-based rock band drawing straight from the legends of the scene. Our debut single found its way onto playlists and radio and blogs across Europe, the UK, and Australia — and out onto stages in all three.

We've shared a bill at Victorious Festival with The Kooks, Biffy Clyro, and Noel Gallagher. Now, with a new release on the way, we're chasing new rooms and new crowds to fill them.

The Fundamentals are the band members. The Fundamentals is the crowd. The Fundamentals is everyone who inspired this journey.
